# Keyword-to-page mapping

| Page | Primary keyword | Secondary keywords | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | ram price | ram price india, ram price today | Commercial |
| `/ram-prices/ddr5` | ddr5 ram price | ddr5 8gb ram price in india, 32gb ddr5 ram price | Commercial / informational |
| `/ram-prices/ddr4` | ddr4 ram price | ddr4 8gb ram price, ddr4 16gb ram price | Commercial / informational |
| `/laptop-ram` | laptop ram price | 8 gb laptop ram price, 16gb ram laptop price | Commercial / informational |
| `/ram-prices/64gb` | 64gb ram price in india | DDR5 64GB RAM | Commercial / informational |
| `/ram-prices/32gb` | ram 32gb | 32gb ddr5 ram price | Informational |
| `/compare/samsung-8gb-ddr4-sodimm` | samsung 8gb ram | samsung 8gb | Informational |
| `/ram-guide/how-much-does-ram-cost-in-india` | ram price india | RAM price today, DDR4 vs DDR5 | Informational |

The homepage introduces broad comparison intent. Generation, capacity and laptop pages address distinct primary intents to reduce keyword cannibalization. Product pages serve narrow product research intent. Natural internal links move users from discovery to comparison, technical understanding and external retailer verification.

Source keyword metrics are preserved in `src/data/keywords.js`.
